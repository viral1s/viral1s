About Viral1s

Creating reels should be simple.

Viral1s is an online reel creation tool built to help creators turn their ideas into short-form videos without dealing with complicated editing software.

What is Viral1s?

Viral1s provides ready-made reel templates that make creating short-form content faster and easier. Instead of starting every video from scratch, creators can choose a format, add their own content, customize it and export the finished reel.

Why we built it

Short-form content moves quickly, but traditional video editors can require unnecessary time and complexity for simple social media formats.

Viral1s focuses on a simpler workflow: choose a template, customize the important parts and create your reel directly from your device.

Built for short-form creators

Viral1s is designed for people creating content for platforms such as TikTok, Instagram Reels and other short-form social media platforms. Our goal is to keep the experience fast, practical and easy to understand on both mobile and desktop.

Privacy-focused editing

Where supported by a template, Viral1s processes reel creation directly in your browser and on your device. This approach can reduce unnecessary server processing while keeping the editing workflow fast and responsive.

Our goal

We want Viral1s to become a useful collection of simple, ready-to-use formats that help creators spend less time configuring editing tools and more time creating content.
