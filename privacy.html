Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters.

This Privacy Policy explains how Viral1s may collect, use and protect information when you visit viral1s.com and use our reel creation tools.

Last updated: September 2, 2026

1. Information you provide

Viral1s does not currently require users to create an account to use the basic reel editor.

When you add text, images or other media to a supported template, that content may be processed directly in your browser and on your device where the template supports browser-based processing.

2. Automatically collected information

Like most websites, certain technical information may be processed automatically when you visit Viral1s. This can include information such as your browser type, device type, IP address, operating system, referring pages and general usage information.

This information may be processed by our hosting, infrastructure or third-party service providers for purposes such as security, reliability, performance and basic website operation.

3. Cookies and local storage

Viral1s may use browser storage technologies such as local storage to remember certain editor preferences, including settings such as hook text, font choices or editing options.

Cookies may also be used by third-party services that are enabled on the website, including advertising, analytics, security or other website services.

4. Advertising and Google services

Viral1s may use third-party advertising services such as Google AdSense in the future.

If Google advertising services are enabled, Google and other third-party vendors may use cookies to serve ads based on a user's previous visits to Viral1s or other websites.

Google's use of advertising cookies may allow Google and its partners to serve advertisements based on visits to this site and other websites on the Internet.

Users can manage personalized advertising preferences through Google's Ads Settings.

5. Third-party technologies

Third-party services used by Viral1s may place or read cookies on a user's browser, or use technologies such as web beacons, IP addresses or similar identifiers when providing their services.

Those third parties process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

6. How we use information

Information may be used to operate and improve Viral1s, maintain website security, troubleshoot technical issues, understand general site usage and provide features requested by users.

7. Uploaded content

You should only upload images, audio, text or other content that you have the right to use.

Where a template processes content locally in your browser, Viral1s does not need to permanently store that media on a Viral1s server in order to generate the reel.

8. Children's privacy

Viral1s is not specifically directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly request personal information from children under 13.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when Viral1s adds new features, services, analytics, advertising technologies or legal requirements.

The latest version will always be published on this page with an updated revision date.

10. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, you can contact Viral1s through the contact information provided on our Contact page.
