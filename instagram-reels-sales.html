3

What should your CTA say?

Keep it clear. Do not ask viewers to do five things at once.

Give one next step.

Depending on the Reel, your CTA could be: “Check the product.” “Visit the store.” “Comment ‘link’.” “Save this for later.” “Follow for more tips.” Match the CTA to what you actually want the viewer to do next.

Do Instagram Reels guarantee sales?

No. Reels can help more people discover your product, but views alone do not guarantee purchases.

Traffic is only one part of the sale.

Your results can also depend on the product, audience, price, offer, trust, website, checkout and customer experience. Use Reels as a free way to test messages, hooks and product angles with real viewers.
