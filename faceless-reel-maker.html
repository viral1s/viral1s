Faceless reel maker FAQ

What is a faceless reel?

A faceless reel is a short video that does not require the creator to appear on camera. It can use text, images, music, product visuals or other media to communicate the idea.

Can I use Viral1s for TikTok and YouTube Shorts too?

Yes. Viral1s creates vertical short-form videos, so the same workflow can be used for Instagram Reels, TikTok and YouTube Shorts.

Do I need advanced video editing skills?

No. Viral1s is designed around a focused workflow instead of a large traditional editing timeline. Add your text, add a visual, preview the reel and export it.

Can I create the visual before opening the reel editor?

Yes. You can use the Viral1s Image Creator to prepare a square visual, then continue in the reel editor.
