7 ways to promote your product for free

You do not need to do everything at once. Pick the methods you can repeat consistently.

1

Instagram Reels

Create short videos around the problems, desires and questions connected to your product. Do not make every Reel feel like an ad.

2

TikTok

Repurpose your strongest short-form ideas instead of creating a completely different video for every platform.

3

YouTube Shorts

Publish quick tips, product benefits, common mistakes and problem-solution videos related to your niche.

4

Helpful SEO content

Create useful pages answering questions potential customers already search for. Search traffic can continue after the post is published.

5

Pinterest

Create useful visual content around your niche, product category and customer problems. Link relevant Pins to useful pages or products.

6

Answer customer questions

Every real customer question can become a Reel, Short, post or article. Useful answers also build trust.

7

Repurpose your best ideas

One strong idea can become multiple pieces of content. Change the hook, visual or angle without starting from zero.

Do not start your content with the product

Start with something your customer already cares about.

Example

Instead of:

“Our product is now available. Buy today.”

Try:

“Still struggling with X? Here is a simpler way.”

Then show the product. The first version asks for attention. The second gives the viewer a reason to pay attention first.
